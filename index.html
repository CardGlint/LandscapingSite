About us

A landscape studio with a feel for place.

VERDE SOL STUDIO is a concept identity for a Mediterranean landscape design studio in Cyprus. We imagine outdoor spaces that respond to the local light, the natural character of the land, and the way people want to spend time outside.

Our approach brings planting, paths, shade, materials, and everyday rituals together into one thoughtful landscape — whether that means rethinking an existing garden or shaping an outdoor space from the ground up.
